We Do Wonder

Inspiring Change in Vietnam through Education and Community.

Founded in 2021, in Danang, Vietnam, We Do Wonder has directly impacted 100+ young children, 50+ youth, and reaching over 100000 students through our initiatives and platform.

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world."
- Nelson Mandela

The Wonder

Our first public program in May 2022. We held 6 English classes for 40 students.
This was made possible thanks to the hard work of 20 members of We Do Wonder team to improve our publicity and 12 volunteers to run the classes <3

Wonderland

In August 2022, with the kind donation from The Wonder program participants, fundraising and bake sale efforts, we were able to raise 10,000,000 VND.

We donated the majority of the fund along side with other donatations of study materials, clothings, etc to the children in Danang Street Children Center. Our team also visited the children center to deliver the donation and to share the story of The Wonder program with them. With the remaining funding, we organized a "Holiday Gift" distribution drive to distribute to the local lower-income workers and families.

We are extremely grateful for the support from our donors and volunteers!
